FAQ

Common questions

How does Elmer work?
Open the app, snap a few photos of your work, and record a short voice description — just talk naturally about what you did. Elmer transcribes your voice, extracts the key details, and writes platform-appropriate posts for each connected channel. You review, edit if you want, and publish.
Do I need to know how to write social media posts?
No. Elmer does the writing for you. If you can describe a job to a customer or a friend, that's all you need. Record your voice, and Elmer handles the rest.
Can I edit posts before publishing?
Yes. Every generated post is fully editable before you publish. Read it over, make any changes you want, and send it when you're ready. Nothing goes out without your approval.
Which platforms can I connect?
Elmer currently publishes to Google Business Profile, Facebook Pages, Threads, and WordPress. Instagram support is coming soon.
Can I use my own photos?
Yes. Take photos right from the app using your camera, or choose existing photos from your library. Up to 5 photos per post. Before-and-after pairs work especially well and can be automatically formatted as a comparison image.
How does the free trial work?
Your trial starts the day you sign up and lasts 14 days. You get full access to everything during the trial. After the trial ends, you're charged $19/month. Cancel before your trial ends and you won't be charged.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account dashboard. No long-term contracts, no cancellation fees.
