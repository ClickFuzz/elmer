How It Works

Finish a job. Share it in a minute.

If you can take a photo and talk about your work, you can use Elmer. No writing, no marketing know-how, no learning curve.

Step 01

Snap Your Photos

Take a few pictures of your finished job right from the app — up to five. Before-and-after shots work especially well. They show the transformation and let the quality speak for itself.

Step 02

Tell Elmer What You Did

Record a quick voice description. No typing, no scripts — just explain the job the way you would to a customer. "Replaced the roof on a two-story in Springfield, full tear-off, architectural shingles." That's enough.

Step 03

Review and Publish

Elmer writes the posts for each platform you're connected to. You read them over, make any edits you want, and publish. That's it — you're back to work, and your latest job is out there working for your business.

You do the work. Elmer spreads the word.
