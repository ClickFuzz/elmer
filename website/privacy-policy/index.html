Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Elmer ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by ClickFuzz LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you use the Elmer mobile application (iOS and Android), the Elmer website at https://getelmer.com, and the Elmer billing portal at https://billing.getelmer.com (collectively, the "Services").

By using the Services you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

Information We Collect

Account and Profile Information

When you create an Elmer account we collect your email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We also store business profile information you provide, including your business name, website, services, service areas, and writing style preferences.

Voice Recordings

The Elmer app allows you to record a voice description of a job. When you request content generation, that recording is transmitted to our server and sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription. We do not retain your audio recording after transcription is complete. The audio file is deleted from our server immediately after the transcription request is processed. The resulting text transcript and any AI-generated content are stored locally on your device and may be retained by us in your publishing history as described below. We do not use voice recordings for voice identification, biometric authentication, or advertising targeting.

Photos

When you publish content through Elmer, photos you select are transmitted to our server for processing (such as aspect-ratio correction) and then forwarded directly to the destination platform (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Google Business Profile, or WordPress). We do not retain your photos on our servers after the publishing request is complete. Photos remain stored on your device in the Elmer app until you delete your publishing history or delete the app. We do not send photo contents to our AI text generation service.

Location Information

When you take photos through the Elmer app, we may extract GPS coordinates embedded in the photo metadata (EXIF data) if location access was enabled when the photo was taken. These coordinates are transmitted to our server as part of the content generation request. If your voice description does not contain a location, we use the GPS coordinates to perform a reverse geocoding lookup via the Nominatim service (powered by OpenStreetMap) to identify an approximate job location. The coordinates are used only within that content generation request and are not stored separately on our servers beyond what is retained as part of your publishing history.

AI-Generated Content

Your voice transcript, business profile, and photo metadata are sent to OpenAI's API (GPT-4o mini) to generate social posts, Google Business posts, and blog articles. The generated content is stored locally on your device and in your server-side publishing history.

Connected Social and Publishing Accounts

When you connect a social platform (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Google Business Profile, or WordPress), we receive and store an OAuth access token or application password issued by that platform. These credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM and stored on our server. They are used only to publish content on your behalf. We do not sell information obtained from connected accounts or use connected account data for third-party advertising. You may disconnect any platform at any time through the app's Settings screen.

Publishing Records

We maintain records of content you have published through Elmer, including the platform, date, post identifier, and outcome status. These records are stored on our server and are associated with your account. You can delete your publishing history at any time through Settings → Data Management in the app.

Subscription and Billing Information

Subscription purchases made through the iOS App Store or Google Play are processed by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. We receive subscription status, entitlement, and renewal information from RevenueCat but do not receive your full payment card information. Subscription purchases made through our web billing portal are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects and processes your payment information and billing address. We receive subscription status and limited customer information from Stripe. For further information, see the privacy policies of Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and Stripe.

Usage and Analytics Data (Mobile App)

The Elmer mobile app uses PostHog, a product analytics service, to collect usage events. PostHog is configured to identify events with your internal Elmer user ID. We do not use PostHog session replay, automatic lifecycle events, or geolocation. Events tracked include actions such as completing account registration, starting the onboarding flow, connecting a social platform, generating content, viewing the subscription paywall, and publishing. No voice content, photo content, or message text is included in these events. For more information, see PostHog's privacy policy.

Our server also uses PostHog to track subscription lifecycle events (trial started, payment processed, subscription cancelled) linked to your internal user ID.

Website and Billing Portal Data

When you visit https://getelmer.com or https://billing.getelmer.com, we may automatically collect certain information including your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and other usage information.

Our website may use analytics and advertising services provided by third parties, including Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Meta/Facebook advertising technologies. These services may collect information about your device, browser, and interactions with our website. You may control cookies through your browser settings or the consent controls provided on the website.

We use information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Elmer Services;
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account sessions;
  • Transcribe your voice recordings and generate content on your behalf;
  • Publish content to connected platforms when you request it;

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Third-party platforms you choose to connect — When you publish through Elmer, we transmit content and credentials to the platform you have selected (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Google Business Profile, or WordPress) on your behalf.
  • AI and processing providers — OpenAI receives your audio recordings (for transcription) and content context (for generation). OpenAI's use of this data is subject to OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Analytics providers — PostHog receives usage event data as described above.
  • Subscription and billing providers — RevenueCat, Apple, Google, and Stripe receive information necessary to process and manage your subscription.
  • Service providers — We use service providers for server hosting, email delivery, and other infrastructure. These providers access information only as necessary to perform services for us.
  • Legal requirements — We may disclose information when required by law, legal process, or governmental request, or when necessary to protect our rights, users, property, or safety.
  • Business transactions — Information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Information sharing to subcontractors in support of services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use-case categories exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from us.

If you consent to receive text messages from Elmer, we may send messages related to your inquiries, appointments, service requests, transactions, account, customer care, or other communications you have requested or consented to receive. If you separately consent to marketing messages, we may also send promotional messages.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at help@getelmer.com.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. OAuth tokens and application passwords for connected platforms are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. All connections to our API use HTTPS. No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

Data Retention

Mobile device. Job content (audio, photos, transcripts, generated posts) is stored locally on your device. The app automatically deletes media files for published jobs after 30 days. Unpublished drafts are kept until you delete them manually. You can clear all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Data Management.

Server. Your account, business profile, connected platform credentials, and publishing records are stored on our servers for as long as your account is active. Voice recordings are deleted from the server immediately after transcription. Photos are deleted from the server immediately after the publishing request is complete. Publishing records are retained until you delete them through the app or delete your account.

Previously published content. Content published to external platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Google Business Profile, WordPress) remains on those platforms after you delete your Elmer account. You can remove previously published content directly on each platform.

Transaction records. Certain billing and transaction records may be retained as required by law or for legitimate accounting and legal purposes even after account deletion.

Account Deletion

You can delete your Elmer account directly within the app by going to Settings → Data Management → Delete Account. If you have an active subscription, you must cancel it before deleting your account. Account deletion permanently removes your user record, business profile, connected platform credentials, session tokens, and publishing history from our servers. Local data on your device is also deleted as part of this process.

You may also request account deletion by emailing admin@clickfuzz.com with the subject line Elmer Data Deletion Request.
