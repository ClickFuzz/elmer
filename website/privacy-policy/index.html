Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

Elmer ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit https://getelmer.com, contact us, submit a form, communicate with us, or otherwise use our website and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, telephone number, address, information submitted through contact or quote-request forms, appointment or service information, and any other information you choose to provide.

We may also automatically collect certain information when you use our website, including your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, interactions with the website, approximate location derived from your IP address, and other usage information.

How We Use Your Information

We may use information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries, requests, and customer service needs;
  • Provide quotes, estimates, appointments, products, or services you request;
  • Communicate with you regarding your inquiry, account, appointment, project, purchase, or service;
  • Send marketing or promotional communications where permitted and where appropriate consent has been obtained;
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services;
  • Understand how visitors use our website;
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising and marketing;
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, security incidents, or other harmful activity; and
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal obligations.

Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, understand website usage, measure advertising performance, and improve our marketing.

We may use analytics and advertising services provided by third parties, including services such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta/Facebook advertising technologies, and similar platforms. These services may collect information about your device, browser, website activity, and interactions with advertisements. Depending on the services enabled, we and third-party providers may use first-party and third-party cookies or other identifiers to measure website activity, understand audiences, provide advertising, measure advertising performance, and support remarketing or similar advertising activities.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings, device settings, or any consent controls provided on this website. You may also use privacy and advertising controls made available by the applicable advertising or analytics providers. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

How We Share Information

We may disclose personal information to service providers and contractors that perform services on our behalf, such as website hosting, customer relationship management, communications, analytics, advertising, payment processing, scheduling, customer support, and other business operations. These providers may access information only as reasonably necessary to perform services for us or as otherwise permitted by law.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, court order, or governmental request; when necessary to protect our rights, customers, property, or safety; in connection with the investigation of fraud or unlawful activity; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets, or similar business transaction.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from us. Where SMS consent is offered, you may choose whether to provide that consent.

Text Messaging

If you consent to receive text messages from Elmer, we may send messages related to your inquiries, appointments, service requests, transactions, account, customer care, or other communications you have requested or consented to receive. If you separately consent to marketing messages, we may also send promotional messages, special offers, or other marketing communications.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at help@getelmer.com.

Opting out of text messaging does not necessarily prevent us from contacting you through other methods where permitted by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting information over the Internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, provide our services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may provide rights concerning your personal information, which may include the right to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails by using the unsubscribe mechanism included in those communications. You may opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP.

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to websites, platforms, or services operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites or services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website. If we learn that we have collected such information without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our practices, technologies, services, or legal requirements. When we update this policy, we may revise the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of the website after an updated policy is posted is subject to the updated policy.
