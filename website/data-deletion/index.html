Privacy & Data

Data Deletion

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Elmer respects your privacy and gives you control over your personal information. You can request deletion of your Elmer account and associated data at any time.

Elmer is operated by ClickFuzz LLC.

Delete Your Account In-App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly in the app:

  1. Open the Elmer app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Data Management.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Elmer user account, business profile, connected platform credentials, session tokens, and publishing history from our servers. Local job data, photos, and recordings on your device are also deleted as part of this process.

Active Subscription Requirement

If you have an active subscription, you must cancel it before your account can be deleted. The app will display an alert with a link to your subscription management page if deletion is blocked by an active subscription.

Important: Deleting your Elmer account does not automatically cancel an active subscription billed through Apple or Google. Cancel your subscription through the platform where you purchased it to avoid future charges.

  • iOS subscribers: Cancel in Apple ID Account Settings → Subscriptions.
  • Android subscribers: Cancel in Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Web subscribers: Cancel through the billing portal at billing.getelmer.com.

After canceling, your subscription access remains until the end of the current billing period. You can delete your account at any time after canceling.

Delete Only Your Publishing History

To remove your server-side publishing records and all locally stored jobs, photos, and recordings without deleting your account:

  1. Open the Elmer app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Data Management.
  3. Tap Delete Publishing History and confirm.

This removes all saved jobs, drafts, photos, and recordings from your device and deletes your publishing history from Elmer's servers. It does not delete your account or disconnect your connected platforms.

Disconnect a Connected Platform

To remove Elmer's access to a specific connected platform without deleting your account:

  1. Open the Elmer app and go to Settings.
  2. Find the connected platform you want to disconnect.
  3. Tap Disconnect.

This removes the stored OAuth credentials for that platform from Elmer's servers. It does not remove content already published to that platform.

You can also revoke Elmer's Meta app permissions directly on Facebook or Instagram:

  1. Sign in to Facebook.
  2. Open Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  3. Navigate to Apps and Websites.
  4. Find Elmer and select Remove.

Revoking permissions prevents further access through that authorization but does not delete data already stored by Elmer. Use the in-app Disconnect option or submit an email request (below) to request deletion of stored data.

Email-Based Deletion Request

If you are unable to use the in-app deletion options, you may request deletion by email:

  1. Send an email to admin@clickfuzz.com with the subject line Elmer Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address associated with your Elmer account.
  3. Specify whether you want your entire account deleted or only data associated with a specific connected service.

We will acknowledge your request, verify account ownership when necessary, and process the deletion without undue delay and within 30 days, subject to any active subscription requirement and applicable legal requirements.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Depending on the services you have used, deletion of your account may include:

  • Your Elmer user account and authentication credentials.
  • Your business profile and settings.
  • Connected social media account credentials and authorization tokens.
  • Publishing history and records stored on Elmer's servers.
  • Locally stored job data, photos, and recordings on your device.

What We Retain After Deletion

Certain information may need to be retained after account deletion:

  • Billing and transaction records — Stripe, Apple, and Google retain payment and subscription records in accordance with their own policies and applicable law. We may retain minimal records as required for legal or accounting purposes.
  • Security and fraud-prevention records — Limited records necessary to investigate abuse, prevent fraud, or protect the service.
  • Anonymized analytics — Aggregated or anonymized usage data that cannot reasonably be re-associated with you.
  • Legal holds — Information retained when required by law, legal process, or governmental request.

Previously Published Content

Deleting your Elmer account does not automatically remove content previously published to external platforms, including Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads, Google Business Profile, or your website. You can manage previously published content directly through the respective platforms.
